      How to Play

      iNaturalist is a citizen science organization where anybody can submit observations of species! In natGuessr, you have to guess the country each of these 5 observations was made in! Every observation was made in the same country, but they may be from different locations. You have 5 guesses! Wrong guesses show how close you are and a compass pointing toward the answer. If you guess wrong twice, you can see the names of the species.

      Thumbnails are cropped to fit, click any image to see it uncropped.

      Proximity is measured between the closest borders of the two countries. Closer guesses are worth partial credit!

      Observations and ranges are taken from iNaturalist, CC BY.

      Report

      All observations are curated by hand. If I've missed something concerning, please send me an email at birdishbrainish@gmail.com. If you would like your observation taken down for any reason at all, please feel free to contact the same email ASAP.
