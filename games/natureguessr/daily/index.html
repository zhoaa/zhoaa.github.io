      How to play

      Play

      Guess the country shared by a bird, mammal, reptile or amphibian, plant, and fungus. You have five guesses. Species names are hidden for your first two guesses and appear before your third. They also appear when the round ends. Wrong guesses show distance between borders and a compass toward the answer.

      Distance boxes are color-coded from blue (cold/far) to red (hot/near). Green means the correct country.

      • Blue: 5,000 km or more
      • Teal: 2,000–4,999 km
      • Gold: 800–1,999 km
      • Orange: 200–799 km
      • Red: under 200 km

      Distance is measured approximately between the closest borders, including islands. Countries sharing a border show “Borders touch”; the compass points toward that border. Each unused guess is worth 1,000 points, up to 5,000 per puzzle.

      Evidence, not certainty

      “Unique” means only one country in our modeled range index supports all five species. Models can omit real occurrences, and borders are approximate. It is not a claim that the five species cannot occur elsewhere.
